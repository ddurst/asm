ASM and Ephemera Obscura present:
 
Infrequent Seams

Infrequent Seams brings quartet improvisations to Brooklyn's up-and-coming Williamsburg neighborhood. Experience a quartet featuring a special guest (who is contractually obligated to not mention their other gig that weekend), performing on traditional and homemade instruments. This ain't your ordinary improv set.

Performing:
James Ilgenfritz on contrabass and objects
AC Diamond on electronics
NAHTAL on woodwinds objects
Special Guest (or two) on we're-not-telling-you-have-to-attend-to-find-out

Monday, June 29, 2026 at 8:00 PM
Scholes Street Studio
375 Lorimer St
Brooklyn, NY 11211
